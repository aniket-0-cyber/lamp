Advent reading plan 2026: 25 chapters from Genesis to the manger

Wicklight Labs · 5 November 2026

Advent begins on Sunday 29 November 2026. This plan reads one chapter a day from 1 December to Christmas Day, following God's promise of a Saviour from Genesis to Bethlehem. It takes about five minutes a day.

How to keep going

  • Read at the same time each day, before your phone gets busy.
  • Missed a day? Read two chapters the next day, or simply carry on. Advent is not a test.
  • Read it with your family: one chapter after dinner, one person reading aloud.
  • Write down one line that stayed with you. By Christmas you will have 25.
