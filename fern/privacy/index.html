Fern Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 October 2026.

The short version

Fern has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and we do not track you. Your health log stays on your iPhone. We never see it, sell it or share it.

Your log

Your medicines, doses, injection sites, symptoms, food, water, weight, measurements and progress photos are stored only on your device. They are not sent to our server and are not stored in iCloud. If you save a backup, it is a file you choose where to keep. Deleting an entry, or the app, deletes it.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, Fern reads your weight and saves the weigh-ins you log in Fern to Health. Health data is used only to show your own charts. It is never used for advertising or shared with anyone. You can change access in the Health app at any time.

Barcodes and nutrition labels

When you scan a barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts, a public food database, to look up the product. Nothing else is sent with it. Photos of nutrition labels are read on your device and are not sent anywhere.

Plate photos (Fern Pro)

If you use a plate photo, the photo is sent to our server, which passes it to Google's Gemini API, a third-party AI service, to estimate the protein and fiber in the food. The photo is used only for that and is not stored on our server. Google processes it under the Gemini API terms and may keep request data for a limited time to detect abuse. A random identifier for your installation is sent with each photo so we can count the day's photos; it is not linked to your name or Apple Account. The app asks for your agreement before the first plate photo.

Reminders

Dose reminders are notifications scheduled on your device. Nothing about them is sent to us.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Fern is meant for adults and is not directed to children.
