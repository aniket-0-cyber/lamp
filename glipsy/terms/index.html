Not medical advice

Glipsy is a personal log. It does not diagnose, treat or prescribe, and it never suggests a dose. Always follow your prescriber's instructions and check with your doctor or pharmacist before changing anything about your treatment. If you have a medical emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Estimates

The medicine level shown in Glipsy is an estimate calculated from the doses you log and published prescribing information. It is not a blood test and varies from person to person. Protein and fiber amounts from barcodes, labels and plate photos are estimates too; check them before you rely on them.

Subscriptions

Glipsy Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, yearly or monthly. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, or at the end of a free trial if one applies. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings. The basic features of Glipsy are free and do not require a subscription.
