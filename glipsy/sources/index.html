Glipsy Medical Sources

Where the medical information in Glipsy comes from. Glipsy keeps a record for you and is not medical advice: check with your doctor before changing anything about your treatment.

How the daily goals are set

  • Protein: about 0.55 g per lb of goal weight (1.2 g per kg), kept between 60 and 140 g. The advisory below names 1.2 to 1.6 g per kg, about 0.55 to 0.73 g per lb, during weight loss.
  • Fiber: 25 g, or 30 g if constipation is a concern, within the 22 to 34 g NIDDK gives for adults.
  • Water: a Glipsy starting point worked out from weight, about 68 to 101 oz (2 to 3 litres) a day, set within the reference ranges below. No guideline gives this exact formula.

These are starting points you can change. Ask your doctor or dietitian what's right for you.

Goals and tips

  • Nutritional priorities to support GLP-1 therapy for obesity. Joint advisory of the American College of Lifestyle Medicine, American Society for Nutrition, Obesity Medicine Association and The Obesity Society. Mozaffarian D, et al. Obesity Pillars, 2025. Used for the protein goal, and for protein with strength exercise to help keep muscle.
  • Managing gastrointestinal side effects of GLP-1 medicines. Multidisciplinary expert consensus. Gorgojo-Martínez JJ, et al. Journal of Clinical Medicine, 2023. Used for the tips on nausea (smaller portions, eating slowly, stopping when full), on drinking enough, and on water and fiber for constipation.
  • Eating, diet and nutrition for constipation. US National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases (NIDDK). Used for the fiber goal (adults: 22 to 34 g a day) and for adding fiber a little at a time, with fluids.
  • Dietary intake levels for water. US National Academies. Adequate total water from all drinks and food: about 91 oz (2.7 litres) a day for women and 125 oz (3.7 litres) for men. Used for the water goal.
  • European dietary reference values for water. European Food Safety Authority (EFSA). Adequate daily intake: about 68 oz (2.0 litres) for women and 85 oz (2.5 litres) for men. Used for the water goal.
  • FoodData Central. US Department of Agriculture (USDA). Used for the protein and fiber in everyday foods in food search, as typical values, rounded. Packaged foods come from Open Food Facts.
