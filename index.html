Wicklight Labs

Small, private apps for iPhone

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Each app does one thing well.

Lamp Bible

Free iPhone Bible app with a daily verse widget for the Lock Screen and Home Screen, the KJV and World English Bible offline, audio Bible, reading plans, a prayer list, Sleep with Scripture, and Ask, AI answers that quote real verses checked word for word. Also in Spanish with the Reina-Valera 1909.

Lamp Lock

Christian app blocker for iPhone built on Apple's Screen Time: chosen apps stay locked until you read a Bible verse, pray or read a chapter, then open for a set time. Strict mode, focus sessions, daily limits, schedules, reading plans, guided prayers, streaks and Circles with friends. No account, no ads.

HoloVault

iPhone TCG card scanner for Pokémon, One Piece, Disney Lorcana and Yu-Gi-Oh! cards: scan one card, a stack or 200 photos, see today's market price and a year of price history, track collection value like a portfolio, open packs with Pack Rip, set checklists, wishlist price alerts and a Christmas list to share. No account, no ads.

Glipsy

A GLP-1 shot tracker with protein and water goals. Coming soon to the App Store.
